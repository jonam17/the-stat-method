## Kassiano 2025 (preacher curl [1], incline curl [1])

**Kassiano W, Costa B, Kunevaliki G, Lisboa F, Stavinski N, Prado A, Tricoli I, Francsuel J, Lima L, Nunes JP, Ribeiro AS, Cyrino ES. Int J Sports Med 2025;46(5):334–343.** https://doi.org/10.1055/a-2517-0509 (PMID 39809454)

Read: the PubMed abstract page (PDF supplied by the maintainer); funding from the ECU repository record
(CAPES and CNPq, Brazil). Conflicts: "The authors declare that they have no conflicts of interest." The full text
was not supplied — the PDF is the PubMed page — so set counts, load, training background and equipment are not
known, and the incline-curl page says so.

| Claim | Where |
|---|---|
| ★ 63 young women, randomised: preacher (n = 30) or incline (n = 33), twice a week for eight weeks | Abstract |
| ★ Proximal elbow-flexor thickness rose more with incline curls (mean difference 0.08 cm, 95% CI 0.02–0.13) | Abstract |

## Maeo 2023 (overhead triceps extension [1], triceps pushdown [1])

**Maeo S, Wu Y, Huang M, Sakurai H, Kusagawa Y, Sugiyama T, Kanehisa H, Isaka T. Eur J Sport Sci 2023;23(7):1240–1250.** https://doi.org/10.1080/17461391.2022.2100279

Read: full text (PDF supplied by the maintainer; open access). Funding: Descente and Ishimoto Memorial
Foundation for the Promotion of Sports Science (grant 2019-20). Conflicts: "No potential conflict of interest
was reported by the author(s)."

| Claim | Where |
|---|---|
| ★ 21 healthy young adults (14 men, 7 women), no systematic resistance training in the previous 12 months | Methods, Participants |
| ★ Within-person: one arm overhead (shoulder 180°), the other at the side (0°); cable machine; elbow 90°–0° | Methods, Training programme; Figure 1 |
| Twice a week, twelve weeks; 5 × 10; 50/60/70% 1RM then +5% when completed; 2 s / 2 s | Methods |
| ★ Whole triceps +19.9% overhead vs +13.9% at the side; long head +28.5% vs +19.6%; lateral + medial +14.6% vs +10.5% | Abstract; Results (MRI, ANCOVA-adjusted) |
| Relative 1RM gains similar (71.4% vs 62.3%, P = 0.285); absolute 1RM always lower overhead | Results |
| The authors call the neutral-position version "often called … triceps pushdown" | Introduction |

Doubt: the Discussion gives the neutral arm's whole-triceps gain as +13.5%, the abstract and Results as
+13.9%. The pages say "about 14%". The pulley position is not stated in the paper; the how-to steps cite the
trial only for arm position and range.
