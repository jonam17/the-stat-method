## [1] Bassett DR Jr, Howley ET. Med Sci Sports Exerc 2000;32(1):70–84

Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10647532/ (PMID from a search result on screen) · journal, volume,
issue and pages from the article's citation line.

**Checks.** Exists: yes. Type: commentary ("Basic Sciences: Commentaries"), the authors' reply in a
published debate with Noakes — the article says only what they argue and conclude. Population: healthy
humans, whole-body exercise, sea level — the scope the authors set for their conclusion; the article
repeats it. Number provenance: the 20–40% / 2.2-fold figures are from one cited study (their ref 72); the
2–3× small-muscle figure from Saltin 1985 (their ref 70); the marathon figures are the authors' own
worked example. Funding: University of Tennessee EPPE Fund; no competing-interests statement (absence
recorded). Superseded: an older review; it is used for settled physiology and the article attributes the
view to the authors.

## [2] Cooper KH. JAMA 1968;203(3):135–138

Link: none — no PMID or DOI was on screen; the maintainer supplied the PDF. Volume, issue and first page
from the printed footer on p. 1 ("JAMA, Jan 15, 1968 • Vol 203, No 3 … 135"); last page 138 from the
footer on p. 4. (The PDF also carries running numbers 201–204 at the top of each page, apparently from
a reprint collection; the journal's own page numbers are used.)

**Checks.** Exists: yes. Population: 115 US Air Force male officers and airmen, average age 22 (range
17–52), "the majority … less than 25". **No women.** Number provenance: the engine's
`vo2maxCooper = (metres − 504.9) / 44.73` is the paper's regression (distance in miles = 0.3138 +
0.0278 × VO2) rearranged and converted to metres — checked arithmetically: 0.3138 × 1609.344 = 505.0 and
1609.344 × 0.0278 = 44.74, matching to rounding. The paper gives r = 0.897 and 75%/90% tolerance limits,
but no standard error of estimate. Funding: none stated (USAF Aerospace Medical Laboratory; absence
recorded). Superseded: still the original source for the test; not replaced as the definition.

## [4] Kodama S, Saito K, Tanaka S, et al. JAMA 2009;301(19):2024–2035

financial disclosures none reported. Superseded: consistent with [5] and summarised by [3].
