## [4] Precision Nutrition, "How to do hand portion math to track your macros" (reviewed March 2026) and "Hand-size portion guide" (handout)

**Read:** both in full, from PDFs the maintainer saved on 2026-11-05. **Type:** guidance from a commercial
coaching company (by Emily Greffenius, reviewed by Brian St. Pierre, MS, RD). Its claim that hand portions are
"95% as accurate" as weighing comes from its own internal research and is **not** used.
